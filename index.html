WELCOME TO YOUR ORBIT

Less thinking.
More listening.

Orbital is a tiny music machine. Every sound is made right here in your browser.

  1. Choose a world. Each has its own mood, notes, and rhythm.
  2. Press play. Or use the space bar when you’re not on a control.
  3. Make a mark. Tap the grid to add or remove notes. Tap a voice name to mute it.
  4. Get a little lost. Change the tempo, add space, or shuffle into something new.

Your session stays on this device. “Share this loop” copies a link with your pattern inside it. The arrow on the artwork saves an image. Audio starts only when you press play.
